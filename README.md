# The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Cybersecurity Strategies

**Author:** Leyla Mammadova, Maria Curie-Skłodowska University in Lublin
**ORCID:** [0009-0001-8871-1063](https://orcid.org/0009-0001-8871-1063)
**Type:** Literature-based research paper (STATUS: university paper / preprint / published in VENUE)

## Summary
This paper examines the role of artificial intelligence in cybersecurity strategies: AI-driven threat detection and prevention, machine learning in cyber defense, AI-based security tools (NGAV, SIEM, UEBA, IDPS), the risks of AI (adversarial attacks, data poisoning, bias, privacy), and the ethical and legal considerations, including the GDPR.
